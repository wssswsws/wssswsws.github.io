Math, statistics, and computer science courses I've taken at Penn before Fall 2026, grouped by semester. Course links lead to public course sites or Penn's course catalog.

## Spring 2026

- **[CIS 1210: Programming Languages and Techniques II](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~cis1210/current/)** — Data structures and algorithms, with an emphasis on designing and analyzing efficient programs.
- **[CIS 1912: DevOps](https://cis1912.org/)** — Practical software delivery workflows, including Linux, containers, cloud infrastructure, and automation.
- **[CIS 2400: Introduction to Computer Systems](https://www.seas.upenn.edu/~cis2400/current/)** — Computer architecture and low-level programming, from machine instructions to C and memory.
- **[MATH 3140: Advanced Linear Algebra](https://catalog.upenn.edu/courses/math/)** — Proof-based linear algebra covering vector spaces, linear maps, and their structure.
- **[STAT 4300: Probability](https://catalog.upenn.edu/courses/stat/)** — Random variables and distributions, expectation, independence, and foundational probability models.

## Fall 2025

- **[CIS 1200: Programming Languages and Techniques I](https://fling.seas.upenn.edu/~cis120/current/)** — Program design and abstraction through functional and object-oriented programming in OCaml and Java.
- **[CIS 1600: Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~cis1600/current/)** — Discrete mathematics and proof techniques for computer science, including induction, combinatorics, and graph theory.
