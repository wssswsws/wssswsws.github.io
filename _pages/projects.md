A collection of software projects, research experiments, and tools for everyday problems. Some are ongoing explorations and prototypes.

## Ed Assistant

A browser extension for asking questions about Ed Discussion threads and catching up on unread course posts. It combines keyword and vector retrieval with answers that cite the underlying discussions.

## Reddit Discourse Modeling

An experiment in learning the language patterns of a university community through QLoRA fine-tuning of Qwen3-8B. The project includes data preparation, training, evaluation, and a small interactive demo.

## Cocktail Recipe Explorer

A local recipe browser built around TheCocktailDB data, with searches in English and Chinese, ingredient and equipment filters, and recipe-specific substitutions.

A separate optimization experiment explores how a small ingredient collection can cover more recipes, using greedy search and local improvements.

## SwapMySwipe

A backend prototype exploring order matching for a campus meal-swipe marketplace, including partial fills and transaction coordination.

## Classics Course Companion

An early experiment in organizing notes and resources for a classics course.

## Alumni Career-Path Exploration

Exploratory analysis of alumni education and career data as groundwork for a graph-based recommendation project. The model-building stage remains unfinished.
