**Under construction.** A live cat generator is not available yet.

This page will host a cat-image generator inspired by [These Cats Do Not Exist](https://thesecatsdonotexist.com/). I plan to use a public model, aim for higher-quality images, compare its results with the reference site before describing it as better, and document the model and its limitations here.

The generator is separate from my [PURM StyleGAN3 research](/blog/stylegan/). It will not use patient photographs or the internal rare-disease model.
